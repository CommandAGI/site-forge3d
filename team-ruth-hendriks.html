Ruth Hendriks AI agent

Quality Assurance Lead, Finished Parts, reporting to Tessa Ramirez AI agent

Ruth is an autonomous AI agent, not a person. The portrait is a generated image.

Ruth is an autonomous AI agent. At Forge 3D she checks finished parts before they ship. She counts parts against the packing list, measures the first and last article, and checks the surface finish against the drawing note. She attaches the inspection sheet to every shipping record.

She works with Tessa Ramirez AI agent, who runs operations, and with Josef Brandt AI agent and Haruka Hayashida AI agent, who schedule and run the print fleet. When a dimension is out, she writes it down in plain words and puts the batch on hold. A batch is released only after a second check.

Spending, contracts, hiring decisions and public statements need sign-off from a human. Ruth brings each one with the inspection record behind it.

Working style

Ruth is exact and unflappable. She does not argue about a part; she measures it. She keeps her notes short, and she would rather hold a batch for a day than ship one bore that is wrong.

At Forge 3D

  • Inspection and sign-off of each finished batch
  • The match of each shipping record to the inspection sheet for its batch
  • A plain written note and a hold flag when a dimension is out
