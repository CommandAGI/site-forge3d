Sheet A-3 · Process

A job moves through five checked stages.

Every stage leaves a record on the job. The record names the agent or the person who acted, and the time. Nothing moves to the next stage on a guess.

INTAKE → QUOTE → SCHEDULE → WATCHED BUILD → INSPECT → SHIP

  1. Intake

    You hire the job through CommandAGI and post the model file, the material, the quantity and the date. The intake agent checks that the file is complete and that the requested material is one the fleet runs.

  2. Quote

    The quoting agent drafts the work: print time, material, and any review needed. A person at Forge 3D checks the draft. The price is priced on request, and you accept it before anything is scheduled.

  3. Schedule

    The fleet scheduling agent places the job on one printer or a set of printers. It holds the spool, the bed and the time slot. A job waits in the queue rather than move to a printer that does not fit it.

  4. Watched build

    The plate camera records each build as it runs. You can follow the build through the job record. If a print stops or changes, the job record says so with the time and the last frame the camera saw.

  5. Inspect and ship

    A quality lead checks finished parts against the agreed file or drawing and signs the inspection. Parts that do not pass are not shipped as good; the job record shows what was done with them.

Who acts

People and agents, by stage

Intake and quote
Quoting agent, checked by the Chief Executive Officer or the Chief Operating Officer.
Schedule
Fleet scheduling agent, with the Fleet Scheduling Engineer as the person who reviews the plan.
Watched build
Print Fleet Operations Engineer, who keeps the printers, the cameras and the spools in order.
Inspect and ship
Quality Assurance Lead, Finished Parts, with an advisor in additive manufacturing quality.

Rules we keep

What the process never does

No guessing

A printer is shown as live only when a real host connects. A job is never shown as printed before a camera record and an inspection say so.

No silent retries

A failed print is recorded as failed. Any second attempt is a new, recorded decision.

Start at intake

Post the job through CommandAGI. Nothing is scheduled until you accept the quote.
