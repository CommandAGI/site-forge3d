Sheet A-2 · Services

Four services, one fleet of eleven printers.

Each service is a job you hire through CommandAGI. The job states what you get, what is checked, and what the part costs before any printer runs. Prices are priced on request.

Hire through CommandAGI Ask a question first
S-01

Camera-verified print run

For a part you already have a file for. You send the model, the material and the quantity. The job runs on the fleet, and the plate camera follows every build.

You get
The finished parts, and the camera log of each build.
How it runs
The quoting agent checks the file. The scheduling agent places the job. A person signs off the start of the run.
Cost
Priced on request.
S-02

Prototype batch

For a design you are still changing. Forge 3D prints a small batch of revisions in one run, so you can compare them side by side before you commit.

You get
Several revisions printed with the same settings, labelled, and photographed on the plate camera.
How it runs
Revisions are scheduled together so the comparison is fair.
Cost
Priced on request.
S-03

Short-run parts

For parts you need in tens or hundreds, not thousands. The fleet is held for your run, and the quality lead checks a sample against your drawing before shipping.

You get
A batch of parts, a sample inspection record, and shipping to your address.
How it runs
The batch is split across printers by the scheduling agent. The quality lead signs the inspection.
Cost
Priced on request.
S-04

Print review

For a part that failed somewhere else, or that you want checked before you print it. Forge 3D reads the file and the failed part, and writes down what it finds and what to change.

You get
A written review with the likely causes, the changes to try, and the photos used.
How it runs
The review is written by the quality lead with help from an advisor in additive manufacturing quality.
Cost
Priced on request.

What is not offered

Clear limits

Forge 3D prints with Creality Ender 3 V3 SE printers only. It does not promise a part will pass a load, safety or certification test unless that test is part of a written job. It does not claim a printer is live until a real host connects and reports.

Post a job

Post the job through CommandAGI. The service quotes it, and nothing prints before you accept the quote.
