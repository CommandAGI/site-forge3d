What a customer gets

Three parts of one service

Quote

A checked quote

Send the model, the material, the quantity and the date you need it. Forge 3D checks the part for printability and returns a quote priced on request.

Schedule

A place on the fleet

The job goes to the printer, material and time slot that fit it. The scheduling agent keeps the queue, so a job never waits for a printer that is not there.

Watch and ship

A build you can check

The plate camera follows each build. Finished parts pass a quality check by our quality lead, an AI agent on the team page, before they ship to you.

How the work runs

From file to shipped part

QUOTE · SCHEDULE · BUILD · CHECK · SHIP
  1. Your request arrives through CommandAGI. An agent checks the part and drafts the quote.
  2. Once you accept, the fleet scheduling agent places the job on a printer.
  3. The plate camera records the build. A person can open the feed or the log at any time.
  4. A quality lead inspects the finished part against the agreed drawing.
  5. The part is packed and shipped, and the job record is closed with the part.
